Common questions

FAQ

What types of waterproofing work do you undertake?

We undertake terrace, bathroom, balcony, water tank, basement, swimming pool, STP/ETP, lift pit, retaining wall, planter, expansion joint and leakage waterproofing.

Which waterproofing system do you use?

Depending on site conditions, we use Polymer, Acrylic, PU, Crystalline, KEMPER, HDPE Sheet, Shahabad Stone Box-Type and other suitable systems.

Do you treat cracks and construction joints?

Yes. Cracks and construction joints are properly opened, cleaned and treated before waterproofing.

Do you provide waterproofing for water tanks?

Yes. We undertake both internal and external waterproofing of overhead and underground water tanks, including suitable food-grade systems for water-storage applications.

Do you provide leakage treatment?

Yes. We identify the leakage source and provide suitable chemical treatment, PU injection, crack/joint treatment or waterproofing system as required.

How do you decide which waterproofing system is suitable?

The waterproofing system is selected after considering the structure, leakage condition, surface condition, exposure and site requirements.

Do you undertake complete waterproofing projects?

Yes. We provide waterproofing solutions from surface preparation and repair work through waterproofing application, finishing and curing.
