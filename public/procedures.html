Terraces and balconies

Terrace waterproofing

  1. Surface preparation

    Surface cleaning and preparation.

  2. Crack repair

    Cracks opening, cleaning and filling with GP2 / Micro Concrete as required.

  3. Chemical coating

    Application of suitable Polymer / Acrylic / PU / other waterproofing coatings.

  4. Mesh / fleece reinforcement

    Fiber mesh / fleece fabric reinforcement at required areas.

  5. Slope and line level

    Proper line, level and drainage slope marking.

  6. 4" red brick work

    4" red brick laying as per required slope.

  7. Coba and finishing

    Waterproofing coba with proper chamfer, line, level and finishing.

  8. Curing

    Proper curing for 10–15 days.

  9. Final inspection

    Final checking of slope, drainage and finished waterproofing work.

Leakage, pools, and planters

Leakage waterproofing and repair

  1. Leakage inspection

    Identify leakage source and affected area.

  2. Surface preparation

    Clean and prepare the affected surface.

  3. Crack / joint treatment

    Open, clean and fill cracks and joints with suitable repair material.

  4. Injection / chemical treatment

    PU injection / suitable chemical treatment as required according to leakage condition.

  5. Waterproofing coating

    Apply suitable PU / Acrylic / Polymer / Crystalline waterproofing system.

  6. Mesh / fleece reinforcement

    Fiber mesh / fleece reinforcement at critical areas wherever required.

  7. Finishing

    Proper surface finishing and detailing.

  8. Final inspection

    Check the treated area for leakage and complete the final finishing.
